# Rendition 01 — Industrial Editorial

The proposed direction was approved and has been implemented as a separate React homepage prototype in [prototype](../prototype/README.md). The local built preview is http://127.0.0.1:4173/ while running.

## What is ready to review

- Real logo; original colour roles; bold Archivo blocks with Manrope supporting text.
- Three opening compositions under the prototype's Tweaks control.
- Tektronix 5 Series B visual study, made from the user's photographs.
- TO-220, TO-247, QFN and SOT-23 model studies with real geometry, Blender sources and GLB downloads.
- Division panels, application guide, customer marks, company proof, downloads and enquiry paths.
- Existing industry and regional detail retained in expandable sections.
- Desktop, tablet and mobile presentations.

The first model pass is visual, not engineering CAD. The attempted manufacturer STEP import returned empty geometry, so the delivered oscilloscope uses independently constructed geometry. See the prototype README for the exact limits and source files.

## What remains

The 26 secondary pages still use the legacy design. Their route compatibility is preserved in the prototype; their new templates, full content migration, enquiry handling, store integration, SEO/prerendering, production asset refinement and Hostinger deployment remain later milestones.

The first review should decide whether the current heading weight and spacing, overall rhythm, product scale and white-led opening are the right basis for that migration. The navy and compact alternatives are available for comparison without changing content.

Validation evidence and reproduction commands are in [the prototype README](../prototype/README.md). Original site files were not changed.
