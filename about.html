Our story, since 1994

A shared beginning.
A lasting commitment.

Silicom began with a group of college friends and a shared interest in electronics. From those early days in 1994, we have grown through long-standing relationships with customers, manufacturers and the engineering community.

Today, we work with global partners, including test and measurement pioneers such as Tektronix, to provide carefully selected products and services to respected institutes, research laboratories and industry. Our focus remains personal: understand the requirement, recommend a dependable solution and support it professionally.
