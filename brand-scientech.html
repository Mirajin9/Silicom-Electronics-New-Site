About Scientech

Scientech lab equipment, supplied and supported in India.

Scientech Technologies, based in Indore, makes technical-education trainers and test instruments used in engineering colleges, polytechnics and skill-development centres.

Its range covers analog and digital electronics, analog and digital communication, antenna, microwave and satellite communication, optical fiber, embedded systems and IoT, and instrumentation and control.
